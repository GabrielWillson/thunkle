How Thunkle Works
Each puzzle has a different rule and direction. The first pattern will be changed by the second pattern to make the third. This is in either rows or columns. There are 6 ways the patterns can interact with each other (operations). It helps to know that the results of the operations are either the right column or the bottom row, good luck!
Thunkle Quotient (TQ)
The TQ is the Thunkle Quotient. This represents the score of the last five daily challenges you have completed. Each daily score is calculated as:

score = (expected time / your time) x 100

A score of 100 means you solved it at the expected pace. Faster scores above 100, slower scores below. Practice modes show a score on completion but do not count toward your TQ.
